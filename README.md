# WePlay: Choo-Choo Taxi 🚕🚂

**A personal shuttle train you control with one button on the shortcut bar, and it rides the Space Exploration space elevator.**

![Choo-Choo Taxi](thumbnail.png)

Turn any locomotive into your own taxi train. Click the shortcut to call it, hop in, pick a station, and it takes you there. When you step out, it drives itself back to its depot so your stations stay clear.

No research, no items, no equipment grid. Just a button.

📦 **Download:** [WePlay: Choo-Choo Taxi on the Factorio mod portal](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/WePlayChooChooTaxi)

---

## Features

* **One button does everything:** register your taxi, call it, and open the station list
* **Call your shuttle train anywhere:** it comes to the nearest train stop or rail next to you
* **Destination picker:** a searchable list of every station, grouped by surface
* **Depots:** the taxi returns home after each ride so it never blocks a station
* **Space Exploration support:** the taxi rides the space elevator between a planet and its orbit
* **Friendly messages:** pop-ups tell you what your taxi is doing

---

## How to use

### 1. Pick your taxi
1. Click a locomotive to open it.
2. With the locomotive window open, click the **Choo-Choo Taxi** button on the shortcut bar (or press **Alt + J**).
3. That train is now your taxi.

### 2. Call it
1. Stand near any rail or train stop.
2. Click the **Choo-Choo Taxi** button.
3. Your taxi comes to the nearest train stop, or to the closest rail if there's no stop nearby.

### 3. Pick a destination
1. Climb in. The station list opens automatically. Click the button while riding to open or close it.
2. Click a station to go there, or type to filter the list and press **Enter** to go to the first match.
3. When you arrive, the taxi switches to manual and waits while you get out.

### 4. Set a depot
1. Open the station list while riding.
2. Click the small **train-stop button** next to a station to make it the depot. Click it again to clear it.

### 5. Going home
* **Automatic:** when you step out after a ride, the taxi drives back to its depot.
* **By hand:** standing next to your empty taxi? Click the button to send it home.

---

## Space Exploration compatibility

* **Calling across the elevator:** if your taxi is on the other side of a space elevator, calling it sends it through the elevator to you.
* **Riding across the elevator:** stations on the connected surface show up in the station list marked "(via space elevator)".
* **One depot for connected surfaces:** a planet and its orbit can share one depot. If the taxi's side has no depot, it rides the elevator back to the depot on the other side.

> Trips cover one elevator hop at a time (a planet and its orbit). A different planet needs its own depot.

Space Exploration is optional. The mod works without it.

## Settings (per player)

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Open destination picker when boarding | On | Opens the station list when you climb into your taxi |
| Return to depot when you step out | On | Sends the taxi home after each ride |
| Play a sound when the taxi arrives | On | A little "ding!" when your taxi shows up |

## Tips

* Want a different taxi? Open another locomotive and click the button.
* Type `/choo-choo-release` in chat to release your taxi so it's a normal train again.
* Your taxi keeps its interrupts, so refueling interrupts still work.
* If your taxi is in a train group, it's taken out of the group so its trips don't change other trains' schedules.
* Want a taxi that looks the part? Try the companion mod **WePlay: Taxi Train**, a half-size taxi locomotive.
