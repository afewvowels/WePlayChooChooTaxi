# WePlay: Choo-Choo Taxi

A personal shuttle train you control from the **shortcut bar**, and it can ride the Space Exploration space elevator.

## How to use
1. **Pick your taxi:** open any locomotive's GUI and click the Choo-Choo Taxi shortcut (or press Alt+J).
2. **Call it:** click the shortcut while standing near track. It stops at the nearest train stop's rail if one is close, otherwise at the nearest rail.
3. **Ride it:** climb in. The destination picker opens. Click a station, or type to filter and press Enter.
4. When you arrive, the taxi switches to manual mode and waits for you.

## Depots
* In the destination list, click the little train-stop button next to a station to make it the taxi's depot for that planet/orbit. Click it again to clear it.
* When you step out after a ride, the taxi drives back to the depot so it doesn't clog the station. If this side has no depot, it rides the space elevator back to the other side's depot, so one depot can cover a planet and its orbit.
* Standing next to your empty taxi? Click the shortcut to send it home.

Click the shortcut while riding to toggle the picker. `/choo-choo-release` releases your taxi.

## Space Exploration
* If your taxi is on the other end of a space elevator (planet <-> its orbit), calling it sends it through the elevator to you.
* While riding, stations on the linked surface show up in the picker as "via space elevator".
* One elevator hop per trip (that's as far as the rails go!).

No research or items needed. Works without SE too.
